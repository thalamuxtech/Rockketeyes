Full description

1554 / 4000 
Rockketeyes is a fast voice game built on the famous Stroop effect. Words like RED appear in the wrong ink, and your job is to say the ink color out loud, not the word you read. It sounds easy. Your brain disagrees.

SUDDEN DEATH
One wrong color, or reading the word instead of the ink, and the round is over. Clear the whole board to post a full score.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap Play, pick a board and start speaking
• Rockketeyes listens for color words and moves on the moment you get it right
• Prefer to stay quiet? Switch to Tap mode and answer with buttons

A BOARD FOR EVERY LEVEL
• Eight ranked boards, from a quick 3×4 warm-up to a brutal 16×16
• Easy, Normal and Hard color sets, plus a colorblind-safe palette
• Personal bests, speed stats and a time-per-word chart after every round

RACE YOUR FRIENDS
Host a Group challenge, share the PIN or QR code, and race everyone on the same board in real time. Great for classrooms, teams and family game night.

GLOBAL LEGENDS
Connect Google to put your best scores on the daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards. Filter by board, difficulty, voice or tap, and by your country.

MAKE IT YOURS
Pick a nickname, a character or emoji avatar and your flag.

PRIVACY FIRST
Rockketeyes never records or stores your voice. Speech is handled by your device's speech recognition service, and the game only uses the color words it hears. No ads. Delete your account and scores at any time in Settings.

Rockketeyes is a game for fun and focus practice. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool.

Release steps

  1. Put the keystore passwords into android/key.properties (git-ignored), then run flutter build appbundle --release. Output: build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab.
  2. In Play Console, create the app (name above, Game, Free) and accept Play App Signing. Your key keyApp.jks becomes the upload key.
  3. Upload the AAB to Internal testing first. After a new personal developer account is created, Play requires a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before production.
  4. Copy the App signing key SHA-1 and SHA-256 from Play Console (Test and release, App integrity) and add them in Firebase (Project settings, Android app com.rockketeyes.app). Without this, Google sign-in and App Check fail on Play installs.
  5. In Firebase App Check, register the Android app with the Play Integrity provider and link the Google Cloud project in Play Console (App integrity).
  6. Fill the listing, graphics and every App content form above, then send the release for review.
