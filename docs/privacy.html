Privacy Policy

Rockketeyes · Thalamuxtech · Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy explains what the Rockketeyes game (Android app and rockketeyes.web.app) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Rockketeyes is published by Thalamuxtech.

What Rockketeyes does

Rockketeyes is a game in which you say the ink color of words aloud. It keeps your scores and shows public leaderboards.

Microphone and speech

During a round the app listens through your microphone so it can recognise color words. Speech is handled by your device's speech recognition service (Android) or your browser's speech service (web). Depending on your device or browser, that service may process audio on the device or on its provider's servers, under that provider's privacy policy. Rockketeyes does not record, store or transmit your audio. The app only receives the recognised words and uses them to play the game. You can also play in Tap mode without the microphone.

Information we store

  • Account ID. An anonymous account ID is created automatically the first time you play.
  • Profile. The nickname, avatar style and country you choose. These are shown publicly on leaderboards and in group rooms.
  • Game results. Board size, difficulty, score, words cleared and timing for each round.
  • Google link (optional). If you connect Google, the Google account ID and email address from your Google sign-in are kept by Firebase Authentication. Your email is never shown to other players. We never see or store your password.
  • Group rooms. When you host or join a group challenge, your nickname, avatar and live progress are shared with the players in that room. Rooms are temporary.

Settings, personal bests and recent history are also stored locally on your device.

How it is used

We use this information only to run the game, check that scores are genuine, prevent abuse and show leaderboards. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and the app shows no ads.

Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting and App Check) on Google Cloud servers in the United States. On Android, App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from the genuine app. Avatars are drawn by DiceBear and flags are served by flagcdn.com. These two services receive only an avatar seed or a country code. All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Retention

Your profile and scores are kept while your account exists. Round records used to validate scores are short-lived. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database straight away, and from Google backups within their normal backup cycle.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings, then Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your profile, nickname, scores, leaderboard entries and account, including any Google link. You can also request deletion by email; see Delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can change your nickname, avatar or country at any time, play without connecting Google, and play in Tap mode without the microphone. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Email us to exercise these rights.

Children

Rockketeyes is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
