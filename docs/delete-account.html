Delete your account

Rockketeyes · Thalamuxtech

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Rockketeyes.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and data, then confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email ismailukman@gmail.com with the subject "Rockketeyes account deletion" and include your nickname (and the Google email you linked, if any). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account ID and any Google sign-in link
  • Your nickname, avatar and country
  • All your scores and round history
  • Your entries on Global Legends leaderboards

Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from copies in Google Cloud backups, which expire within their normal backup cycle. Group rooms are temporary and are closed by their hosts.
