Privacy Policy

Rockketeyes · Last updated 25 September 2026

What Rockketeyes does

Rockketeyes is a game in which you say the ink color of words aloud. It keeps a leaderboard of scores.

Microphone and speech

During a round the app listens through your microphone so it can recognise color words. Speech is handled by your device's speech recognition service (Android) or your browser's speech service (web). Depending on your device or browser, that service may process audio on the device or on its provider's servers. Rockketeyes does not record, store or transmit your audio. The app only uses the recognised color words to play the game.

Information we store

  • An anonymous account ID created automatically when you first play.
  • The nickname, avatar style and country you choose. These are shown publicly on the leaderboards.
  • Game results: board size, score, words cleared and timing.
  • If you choose to link Google, the Google account ID used for sign-in. We do not store your password.

Settings and personal bests are also stored locally on your device.

How it is used

We use this information only to run the game, validate scores and show leaderboards. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Service providers

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting). Avatars are generated by DiceBear and flags are served by flagcdn.com. These services receive only an avatar seed or a country code.

Your choices

You can change your nickname or country at any time in the app. To delete your account and scores, contact us (see below) and include your nickname.

Children

Rockketeyes is intended for players aged 13 and over.
